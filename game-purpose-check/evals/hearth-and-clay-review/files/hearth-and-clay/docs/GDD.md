# Hearth & Clay: Game Design Document

Version 1.2. Last edited 2026-03-02.

## Purpose

Hearth & Clay is a quiet pottery-shop game for tired adults. You shape clay on a wheel,
fire it, and sell it to villagers whose stories slowly unfold. A session should leave you
calmer than when you started.

## Player

Adults aged 25-45 who play for 15-20 minutes to wind down after work. They like Unpacking,
A Little to the Left, and Spiritfarer. They bounce off anything that stresses them.

## Pillars

1. **Tactile.** The wheel is the game. Shaping clay must feel good on its own, even with
   nothing else around it.
2. **Gentle.** No fail states, no timers, no combat. Nothing you do can be punished. A
   cracked pot is just a funny pot.
3. **Small stories.** 12 villagers, each with a 5-part story told through what they order.

## Core loop

Dig clay > shape on wheel > glaze > fire > sell > hear the next part of a story > repeat.

## Success

- Playtesters in the target group say they feel calmer after a session.
- A typical session lasts 15-20 minutes and players come back the next day.
- Steam reviews use words like "relaxing" and "cozy".

## Limits

Solo developer, evenings and weekends, about 10 hours a week. Art by me, music licensed.
Release target: Steam, October 2027.

## Out of scope

Multiplayer, tech trees, combat, procedural generation.
