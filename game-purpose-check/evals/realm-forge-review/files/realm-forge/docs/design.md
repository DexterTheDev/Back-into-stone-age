# REALM FORGE: Master Design Doc

## Vision

Realm Forge is the ultimate open world sandbox survival MMORPG. Think Minecraft meets Dark
Souls meets Stardew Valley meets World of Warcraft. Something for everyone, casual and
hardcore!

## Target audience

Everyone. Casual players, hardcore players, kids, adults, PvP fans and PvE fans.

## Features

- 10 km2 procedural world with biomes and weather
- 1000 players per server
- 500+ crafting recipes
- Base building and housing
- PvP, guilds, guild wars
- Dungeons and raid bosses
- Pets, fishing, farming
- Skill trees and classes
- Player driven economy with auction house
- Voice acted story campaign
- Mod support
- Console ports

## Timeline

- Month 1-2: core systems
- Month 3-4: multiplayer
- Month 5-6: content
- Month 6-9: launch on Steam
