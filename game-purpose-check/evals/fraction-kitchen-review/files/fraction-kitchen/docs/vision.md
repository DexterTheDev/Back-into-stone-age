# Fraction Kitchen: Vision

Last edited 2026-08-20.

## Why this exists

Fourth graders who fall behind on fractions tend to stay behind in math. Worksheets do not
build number sense. Fraction Kitchen lets kids feel what 3/8 is by cutting and combining food.

## Who it is for

Players: 9-10 year olds in fourth grade classrooms, on school Chromebooks.

Gatekeepers: teachers. A tool a teacher cannot see into does not get used, so the class
progress view is part of the product, not an extra.

## Experience

A ten-minute session where a kid fills kitchen orders by cutting, combining, and comparing
fractions, using visual models first and symbols later. Mistakes show up on the food, with
no penalty.

## Pillars

1. **Visual first.** Every fraction is something you can cut or pour before it is a number.
2. **Safe to be wrong.** No lives, no public scores.
3. **Fits a class period.** Ten-minute sessions, low-end Chromebooks, no installs, no student
   accounts or personal data.
4. **Teacher trust.** Class progress at a glance and an exportable report.

## Success

- Post-quiz scores rise at least 10 percentage points over pre-quiz in pilot classes.
- Teachers choose to use it a second week without being asked.

## Limits

Two people. Grant from the Lakeshore Education Foundation ends June 2027. 12 levels at launch.
