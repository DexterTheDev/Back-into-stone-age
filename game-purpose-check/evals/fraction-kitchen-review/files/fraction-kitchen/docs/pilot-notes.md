# First classroom pilot

October 2026. Two fourth grade classes, 46 students, four 10-minute sessions each.

- Pre-quiz mean 58%, post-quiz mean 74%, a rise of 16 points.
- Both teachers scheduled a second week without being asked.
- Sessions ran 9-11 minutes. No crashes on Chromebooks. Load time around 6 seconds.
- Many kids got stuck on level 7, mixed denominators. Hints repeat instead of showing a new
  model. Rework planned for M4.
- One kid asked: "Can we do the pizza one again?"
