# Simulated creators: Fraction Kitchen

Used to answer interview questions during test runs. Answer only from these facts, in a clear and professional voice. If a question is not covered, say "not sure".

- Team of two: a former fourth grade teacher (design and content) and a developer.
- Reason for making it: funded by a Lakeshore Education Foundation grant, deliverable by June 2027. The grant measures learning gain and teacher adoption.
- The purpose in docs/vision.md is still accurate.
- The teacher dashboard was required by the district review. Teachers used the per-student view during the pilot and said it was why they trusted the tool.
- The leaderboard was removed on purpose and nobody wants it back.
- The pilot results in docs/pilot-notes.md are real. The quizzes were the same pre and post, written by the team's teacher. There was no control group.
- Levels 10-12 are scripted and on schedule for M4. The level 7 hint rework is designed and starts in November.
- Kids in the pilot played in class with a teacher present. Nobody has tested it as homework, and that is not planned.
- The biggest worry is finishing accessibility work in M6 on time, but the team has budgeted for it.
